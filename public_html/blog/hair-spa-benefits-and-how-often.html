Hair Spa Benefits & How Often You Should Get One

The secret to strong, shiny and healthy hair

S.Sense Salon & Spa September 11, 2026

A hair spa is not just a luxury — it is an essential part of maintaining healthy hair. From deep conditioning to scalp detoxification, a professional hair spa treatment can transform dull, damaged hair into soft, shiny locks. Here is everything you need to know.

What a hair spa does for your hair

  • Deep conditioning

    Unlike regular conditioners that coat the surface, a hair spa penetrates deep into the hair shaft, restoring moisture and repairing damage from within. The result is softer, more manageable hair.

  • Scalp detoxification

    Your scalp accumulates product build-up, oil and pollution over time. A hair spa cleanses the scalp thoroughly, unclogs follicles and creates a healthy environment for hair growth.

  • Reduces hair fall

    By nourishing the roots and improving blood circulation to the scalp, hair spa treatments strengthen hair from the root, reducing breakage and hair fall significantly.

  • Adds shine and volume

    The deep nourishment and hydration restore your hair's natural lustre. Hair looks fuller, shinier and healthier after just one session.

“A hair spa is not just a luxury — it is an essential part of maintaining healthy hair. Deep conditioning penetrates where regular conditioners cannot.”

— S.Sense Salon & Spa

How often should you get a hair spa?

  • Normal hair

    Once every 4 to 6 weeks is ideal for maintaining healthy, well-nourished hair. This keeps your hair in top condition without over-treating it.

  • Damaged or coloured hair

    Every 2 to 3 weeks is recommended. Coloured and chemically treated hair needs more frequent nourishment to repair damage and maintain colour vibrancy.

  • Oily scalp

    Every 3 to 4 weeks with a focus on scalp cleansing and oil control. Avoid heavy masks and opt for lightweight, balancing treatments.

  • Dry, frizzy hair

    Every 3 to 4 weeks with deep moisture-rich treatments. Look for treatments with argan oil, keratin or shea butter for maximum hydration.
