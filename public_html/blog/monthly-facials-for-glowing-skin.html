Why Monthly Facials Are Essential for Glowing Skin

Why regular facials make a real difference

S.Sense Salon & Spa August 3, 2023

Facials are not just an occasional treat — when done regularly, they become one of the most effective ways to keep your skin healthy, calm and glowing. At S.Sense Salon & Spa in Kandivali East, our skin specialists recommend a monthly facial to keep your complexion clear and your skin balanced between visits.

What a regular facial does for your skin

  • Deep cleansing

    Professional facials remove impurities, oil and dead skin cells far more thoroughly than daily cleansing, helping to keep pores clear and reduce congestion.

  • Improved circulation

    The massage and treatment steps boost blood flow to the skin, which supports a healthy, natural radiance and a more even tone.

  • Better product absorption

    Exfoliated, freshly prepared skin absorbs your serums and moisturisers more effectively, so your daily routine works harder for you.

  • Long-term skin balance

    Consistent monthly care helps manage dryness, oiliness and sensitivity, keeping your skin calm and comfortable all year round.

“Facials are not just an occasional treat — when done regularly, they become one of the most effective ways to keep your skin healthy, calm and glowing.”

— S.Sense Salon & Spa

Combined with daily cleansing, hydration and sun protection, a monthly facial keeps your skin looking and feeling its best. Our specialists will tailor each treatment to your skin type and concerns.
