Skincare Tips for Mumbai's Humid Weather

Keep your skin fresh and glowing through every season

S.Sense Salon & Spa September 10, 2026

Mumbai's humid climate can wreak havoc on your skin — causing excess oil, breakouts and that uncomfortable sticky feeling. But with the right routine, you can keep your skin fresh, hydrated and glowing all year round. Here are expert tips from S.Sense Salon & Spa in Kandivali East.

Common humid-weather skin problems

  • Excess oil and shine

    Humidity triggers your sebaceous glands to produce more oil, leading to a greasy T-zone and mid-day shine. Using a gentle, foaming cleanser twice a day helps control this without stripping your skin.

  • Breakouts and clogged pores

    Sweat mixed with pollution and oil creates the perfect environment for pimples. Always cleanse your face after being outdoors and use non-comedogenic products that won't clog your pores.

  • Fungal infections

    Mumbai's damp weather can lead to fungal skin infections, especially in skin folds. Keep your skin dry, wear breathable fabrics and use antifungal powders if needed.

  • Dull, dehydrated skin

    Even in humidity, your skin can be dehydrated from air conditioning. Drinking enough water and using a lightweight, water-based moisturiser keeps your skin plump without feeling heavy.

“Even in humidity, your skin can be dehydrated. A lightweight, water-based moisturiser keeps your skin plump without feeling heavy.”

— S.Sense Salon & Spa

Your monsoon-proof skincare routine

  • Cleanse gently, twice daily

    Use a mild, pH-balanced cleanser morning and night. Avoid harsh soaps that strip your skin's natural barrier and trigger even more oil production.

  • Use a lightweight moisturiser

    Switch to gel-based or water-based moisturisers instead of heavy creams. They hydrate without adding greasiness and absorb quickly in humid conditions.

  • Never skip sunscreen

    UV rays穿透 through clouds, even on rainy days. Use an SPF 30+ sunscreen every morning — opt for a matte or gel formula to avoid extra shine.

  • Exfoliate weekly

    A gentle exfoliation once or twice a week removes dead skin cells, prevents clogged pores and helps your skincare products absorb better.

If you are dealing with persistent skin issues from the Mumbai climate, a professional facial can help. Our skin specialists will analyse your skin and create a customised treatment plan.
