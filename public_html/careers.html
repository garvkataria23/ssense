MORE THAN A JOB

What this appointment is designed for

S.Sense Salon & Spa is building a dependable Kandivali East team across hair, makeup, nails and guest relations. The first conversation helps us understand your experience, preferred role, portfolio and schedule so we can match you with the right salon opportunity.

  • Understand your salon craft, service strengths and guest-care approach.
  • Review portfolio work for hair, makeup, nail or beauty roles where relevant.
  • Discuss hygiene standards, timing expectations and the S.Sense service culture.
  • Guide you on the next step only when there is a suitable opening.
